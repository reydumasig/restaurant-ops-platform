# Time Clock Guide

For: **Everyone** — every role clocks in/out for their own shift. The Attendance Report section at the end is for Branch Managers, Commissary Staff overseeing their team, Owner, and Admin.

## 1. Clocking in and out

1. Go to **Time Clock** in the top navigation.
2. If you can see more than one branch (Owner/Admin only), pick the correct branch first — your punch is recorded against whichever branch is selected. Everyone else has their branch selected automatically.
3. Tap **Clock In** at the start of your shift.
4. When you leave — for a break or for the day — tap **Clock Out**, then pick why:
   - **Short Break**
   - **Lunch**
   - **End of Shift**
5. Tap **Clock In** again when you're back from a break to resume your shift.

The button always shows your current state — if it says **Clock In**, you're clocked out; if it says **Clock Out**, you're currently clocked in. **Recent Punches** below the button lists your last several clock in/out times so you can double check you actually punched.

## 2. What this does and doesn't do

- This records **when** you worked, nothing else. There is no payroll calculation in this version — no overtime, no holiday pay, no computed wages. It's a time log, not a payroll system.
- There's no correction request or approval flow yet — if you forgot to clock out, or clocked the wrong thing by mistake, tell your manager so they can note it; there's no self-service "fix my punch" button in this version.
- Location isn't checked or required — the system doesn't verify you're physically at the branch when you punch.

## 3. Attendance Report (Branch Manager, Commissary Staff, Owner, Admin)

Go to **Reports → Attendance** to see everyone's hours for your branch (Owner/Admin can also view any branch, or all of them).

Each row is one shift: employee, branch, date, clock in/out times, and hours worked. A shift shows **Incomplete** instead of a number of hours when someone clocked in but never clocked out (forgot, or a break that was never resumed) — worth following up on Incomplete rows directly with that person rather than guessing at their hours.

Like the other reports, this is sortable by column and exportable via **Export CSV** or **Print / Save PDF**.
